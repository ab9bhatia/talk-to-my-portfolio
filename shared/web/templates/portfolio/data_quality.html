How reconciliation works

Two values, one audited decision

No source value is overwritten
1 · Broker snapshotWhat your broker reported

Position value, quantity, price, and timestamp from the broker sync or imported statement. This remains immutable source evidence.

2 · Independent markWhat the position is worth now

Canonical quantity × sourced market price, with dated FX conversion where required. The source and as-of time are retained.

3 · ResolutionExplain the difference

Review identity, timestamp, quantity, FX, and corporate actions. Saving a resolution appends an audited override; it never deletes the original values.
